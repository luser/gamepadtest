
## Gamepad API Test V1.1
This is a simple demo using the [Gamepad API](http://www.w3.org/TR/gamepad/) in JavaScript. It attempts to account for differences in implementations of the unfinished spec, as well as Chrome's prefixed implementation.

### Version 1.0 - source repository
Written in 2013 by Ted Mielczarek *et al* <ted@mielczarek.org>
(https://github.com/luser/gamepadtest)

### Important note:
Depending on the controller, you may see axes or buttons that do not exist.

Some joysticks may show buttons and axes that don't exist but who may have been intended for other models of the joystick or for future expansion.  Manipulating the buttons and axes will show you what is actually available.

### Example:
The Saitek/Logitech X-52 HOTAS Joystick controller shows ten axes, (0 to 9). Axes 7 and 8 do not exist and may have been included for future expansion or an advanced model that never existed. Axis 9 is the POV switch and does not work like a normal axis.
